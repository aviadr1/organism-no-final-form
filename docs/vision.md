# Vision — a body you build by eating

**Status:** creative direction for ORGANISM — No Final Form. The browser prototype implements the foundation; aspirations below are not a claim of completed features.

## The fantasy

You are the outbreak. A hound's jaw, a worker's face and a stag's antlers should remain recognizable after you consume them. They move with you, distort, acquire new functions and become weapons. The player should remember where a powerful organ came from.

Start as something vulnerable and repulsive. Become a crawling, branching ecosystem. Spend yourself to kill; reclaim what dies to grow. Leave living defenses behind, send offspring into the dark and decide how much of your body you can afford to throw away.

The desired emotional rhythm is hunger, discovery, revulsion, ingenuity, then overwhelming power interrupted by the fear of running out of flesh. Horror and playfulness can coexist: the configurations can be grotesque, surprising and absurd without losing combat readability.

## Design pillars

1. **You are what you ate.** Growth changes the visible anatomy, not just a numeric health bar. Reuse prey anatomy in corpses and grafts so assimilation is legible.
2. **Every part contributes.** Each organ owns its weapon and upgrade level. Mixed weapons attack concurrently. Selection edits a part; it does not silence the rest of the organism.
3. **Growth has a shape.** A mostly linear body trails and bends, with occasional forks. It should feel like flesh under tension, with branching arms rather than a uniform orb or a rigid train.
4. **Biomass creates decisions.** Health, ammunition, offspring and infrastructure compete for the same material. Feeding and reclaiming offspring complete the loop.
5. **Become an ecosystem.** The mobile organism and rooted colonies support different strategies. Expansion should create reasons to travel, return and defend.
6. **Readable horror.** Pixel art must communicate prey, friendly organs, threats, resource remains and incoming attacks even when the body becomes enormous.

## References and translation

| Reference | Direction to carry forward |
| --- | --- |
| The Thing | Anatomical incompatibility, recognizable tissue in impossible arrangements, transformation |
| Slither | Parasitic infestation, wet surfaces, larvae and spread through a population |
| Carrion | Playing the monster, expressive tendrils, movement and impact as body language |
| Pathogenic (user reference) | Infection and mutation mood; a precise mechanical comparison remains unspecified |
| Snake / snake.io | Growth, trailing motion, readable control of a long body |
| Nova-like space shooters | Distributed weapons, orbiting satellites and independent drones; no particular title's rules are assumed |
| Tower defense | Spatial investment, rooted defenses and overlapping areas of control |
| Roguelites | Different builds emerging during each run; permanent progression is a future decision |

Use the references to discuss pacing and feeling. Build an original organism, setting, names and assets.

## Target experience

The first minute teaches movement, feeding and visible assimilation. Soon the player notices different weapons firing from different parts, opens the workshop and improves a favorite organ. Later, colonies and hunters turn one body into a network. A mature run should produce a recognizable personal build rather than only more screen clutter.

The current onboarding starts with several armed parts and three nearby remains. The first-hour experience, campaign structure and intended full-run duration are not established yet.

## Product direction

Keep the browser prototype quick to open and easy to change. Use it to validate whether body ownership, simultaneous weapons and biomass spending are fun before investing in a port. Steam is a possible distribution destination; an engine and desktop packaging path are not selected. Roblox would be a separate adaptation. Multiplayer would introduce shared economy, authority and performance questions, not just another menu option.

The long-term aspiration is a distinctive action game with territorial strategy and mutation-driven builds. The near-term priority is making the creature satisfying to move, read, feed and configure.
