## Individual organ levels

Each organ starts at level 1 and caps at level 5. Upgrades cost 12/24/36/48g. Each added level gives +35% base damage, +8% base range and shot cost, and +12% base firing speed. Equipment changes do not reset the organ’s level or cooldown. See the [GDD](GDD.md#7-upgrades-and-mutations) for formulas.
